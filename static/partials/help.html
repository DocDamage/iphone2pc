Four-step setup

Step 1: Plug iPhone into PC via USB Cable

Use a genuine Apple USB-Lightning or USB-C cable connected directly to a USB port on your PC.

Step 2: Tap "Trust This Computer" on iPhone

Unlock your iPhone. If a popup prompts "Trust This Computer?", tap Trust and enter your iPhone passcode.

Step 3: Verify Apple USB Drivers

Ensure iTunes or the Apple Devices app is installed on Windows to provide the official Apple USB driver service (usbmuxd).

Step 4: Click "Reconnect" in the header

Once your iPhone is plugged in and trusted, click the Reconnect button above to establish the AFC connection.
