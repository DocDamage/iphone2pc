PocketDock Branding Pack
===========================

This pack contains the PocketDock branding renders separated, alpha-cleaned, tightly cropped, and organized for app and marketing use.

Folders
-------
01_primary_logos
  Full horizontal, stacked, compact, and wordmark lockups.

02_brand_marks
  Framed app mark, badge mark, and unframed phone-to-desktop symbol.

03_components
  Icon and wordmark portions separated from the horizontal and stacked lockups.

04_app_icon_sizes
  Transparent square PNG exports in 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128, 144, 180, 192, 256, 384, 512, and 1024 pixels.
  Includes framed and unframed variants plus a multi-resolution favicon/Windows ICO.

05_source_canvases
  Original generated canvases and the user-provided reference logo. The original reference image is opaque; the cropped production PNGs are transparent.

06_preview
  A checkerboard contact sheet for reviewing the main assets.

Notes
-----
- Production PNG files use transparent backgrounds.
- Ultra-low-alpha generation noise was removed before cropping.
- Full-resolution assets retain their native generated resolution; app-icon folders contain resized derivatives.
- The glowing marks read best over dark, navy, gray, or neutral UI surfaces.
